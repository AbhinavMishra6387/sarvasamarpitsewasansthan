About Us

A Trust Rooted in Faith, Devoted to People

Sarva Samarpit Sewa Sansthan is an officially registered charitable trust headquartered at Shree Bade Hanuman Ji Temple, Sangam Marg, Prayagraj.

Sacred Origins & Akhand Sewa

Established under the eternal blessings of the sacred Triveni Sangam and Lord Hanuman, Sarva Samarpit Sewa Sansthan functions on the timeless Vedic tenet: "Nar Seva Narayan Seva" — serving humanity is the highest offering to the Divine.

Operating 24 hours a day, 365 days a year without pause, the Sansthan feeds thousands of pilgrims, sadhus, and indigent families through our Annapurna Kitchen, provides free diagnostics and lifesaving medicines, and protects vulnerable elders during harsh climatic extremes.

Registration

Registered Public Charitable Trust under Indian Trusts Act, 1882 (Govt of UP Reg: UP/2018/0192847).

Tax Exemption

Recognized under Section 12A and Section 80G of the Income Tax Act, 1961 for 50% tax rebate.

Transparency

Annually audited financial records and verified electronic Form 10BD filings.

Our Mission

To maintain an unceasing pipeline of warm, wholesome food, free emergency medical triage, blankets, and sacred dignity for every pilgrim, destitute person, and sadhu in the holy city of Prayagraj.

Our Vision

"A compassionate society where seva is a way of life."

We envision an India where service to the underprivileged is celebrated as an integral dimension of everyday life, inspiring youth and communities to practice universal compassion and collective welfare.

Message from the Founder

Sewa As The Highest Form of Worship

“Sewa is the highest form of worship. When we serve people, we serve the divine.”

Dear Devotees, Well-Wishers, and Volunteers,

When you stand on the banks of Triveni Sangam, where the holy Ganga, Yamuna, and mystical Saraswati merge, you experience the timeless pulse of Bharat. Here, millions arrive in search of spiritual peace. Yet, among them are also those shivering in winter nights without a blanket, those who have traveled hundreds of miles on empty stomachs, and elderly pilgrims unable to afford basic medicines.

It was from this sacred realization that Sarva Samarpit Sewa Sansthan took birth. Our mission is pure: to be the hands that feed, the medicine that cures, and the warmth that consoles.

I invite each one of you to join hands with us. May the blessings of Shree Bade Hanuman Ji protect and inspire you and your family.
