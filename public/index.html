About Us

A Trust Rooted in Faith, Devoted to People

Sarva Samarpit Sewa Sansthan is an officially registered charitable trust headquartered at Shree Bade Hanuman Ji Temple, Sangam Marg, Prayagraj.

Sacred Origins & Akhand Sewa

Established under the eternal blessings of the sacred Triveni Sangam and Lord Hanuman, Sarva Samarpit Sewa Sansthan functions on the timeless Vedic tenet: "Nar Seva Narayan Seva" — serving humanity is the highest offering to the Divine.

Operating 24 hours a day, 365 days a year without pause, the Sansthan feeds thousands of pilgrims, sadhus, and indigent families through our Annapurna Kitchen, provides free diagnostics and lifesaving medicines, and protects vulnerable elders during harsh climatic extremes.

Core Strategic Pillars

Our Key Focus Areas (प्रमुख कार्यक्षेत्र)

The fundamental seven dimensions through which Sarva Samarpit Sewa Sansthan creates enduring ground-level impact:

Education & Knowledge

Free evening pathshalas, distribution of school kits, stationery, uniforms, and moral values coaching for slum and village children.

Service & Support for Needy

24-hour Akhand Annapurna Langar at Shree Bade Hanuman Ji Temple, warm winter blankets, clothing drives, and disaster relief.

Healthcare & Welfare

Regular free medical awareness camps, diagnostic checkups, doctor consultations, and distribution of essential medicines.

Women & Child Empowerment

Sanitary pad distribution drives, menstrual hygiene education, nutritional support for mothers, and self-defense & skill training.

Environmental Protection

Mass tree plantation drives along the Sangam banks and rural belts, river cleaning, plastic awareness, and water conservation.

Social Harmony & Humanity

Promoting universal brotherhood (“Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam”), inter-community solidarity, and marriage support for needy families.

Nation Building (राष्ट्र निर्माण)

Fostering active citizenship, patriotic youth leadership, cultural heritage preservation, and selfless dedication to building a developed, self-reliant India (Atmanirbhar Bharat).
